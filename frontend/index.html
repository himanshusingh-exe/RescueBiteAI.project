How It Works

Four steps to rescue food

From surplus to served in minutes — our AI pipeline handles every step automatically.

01

Upload Food

Restaurant staff photograph surplus food using our mobile app. AI identifies items instantly via computer vision.

02

AI Analysis

Our model estimates servings, predicts safe consumption windows, and generates quality and priority scores automatically.

03

Smart NGO Matching

Intelligent algorithms match donations to the most suitable NGOs based on capacity, proximity, and beneficiary needs.

04

Food Delivered

NGOs receive real-time alerts, confirm pickup, and track delivery — end-to-end transparency on every rescue.

Platform Features

Intelligence at every step

Eight AI-powered modules work together to make every food rescue smarter, faster, and more impactful.

Vision AI

AI Food Recognition

Computer vision model identifies food items from photos with 96% accuracy across 500+ food categories.

ML Model

Serving Estimation

Automatically estimates the number of servings based on portion size, container volume, and food density.

Predictive

Safe Consumption Prediction

Predicts optimal consumption windows by analyzing food type, preparation method, and environmental factors.

Scoring

Donation Quality Score

A 0–100 quality index that factors freshness, nutritional value, and presentation for transparent reporting.

Priority

Rescue Priority Score

Urgency scoring based on perishability, remaining safe time, and donation volume to prioritize pickups.

Matching

Smart NGO Recommendation

Multi-factor matching algorithm recommends the top 3 NGOs ranked by capacity, distance, and beneficiary fit.

Forecasting

Food Waste Prediction

Forecasts future surplus patterns using historical data, enabling restaurants to proactively reduce waste.

Impact

Environmental Impact Dashboard

Tracks CO₂ savings, water conservation, and SDG alignment metrics in a real-time sustainability dashboard.

About Us

Built by engineers who care about food and the planet

RescueBite AI was developed as a final-year AI engineering project to address one of the world's most pressing problems: one-third of all food produced globally is wasted, while 828 million people go to bed hungry every night.

Our platform combines computer vision, natural language processing, and machine learning to create an end-to-end food rescue system that's fast, intelligent, and built for real-world impact.
